# Repairs helper design

The app serves busy repair counters under normal shop lighting. White surfaces, clear black primary actions, the geometric panda-and-wrench symbol, picture tiles and touch-sized controls keep the next task easy to find. Counter is Home in both display modes; Shop overview remains a separate destination.

The public homepage takes its organization from the user’s Square and Stripe references. Its centered Instrument Serif hero has paired calls to action above a wide, self-hosted phone-repair film. Manrope provides consistent typography for navigation, product sections and forms. The display serif is reserved for the hero. Keep headings short, spacing deliberate and the product easy to explore.

A keyboard-accessible product explorer connects Counter, repairs, sales, stock and shop overview. Customer drop-off and pickup scenes are clearly illustrative; app screenshots use fictional demo records. Avoid invented dashboards, metrics, reviews and customer claims. A simple panda head with a blue wrench forms the shared scalable logo; avoid cartoon detail and shading.

Use white, cool neutral (#f4f6f8), dark ink (#111827), and blue (#2563eb). Blue buttons and small text use darker variants for contrast. The assistant section uses navy (#13243d). Sections share a continuous page rather than repeated rounded panels. Screenshots remain uncropped and legible.

Landing styles stay scoped under `.site`. Hero height follows its content. The video has a pause control and a responsive poster; reduced motion, data saver and slow connections use the still image. Mobile navigation closes on selection or Escape. Preserve semantic headings, visible focus and the GET email-prefill signup form.
