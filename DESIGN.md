# Repairs helper design

The app serves busy repair counters under normal shop lighting. White surfaces,
clear black primary actions, the blue helper badge, picture tiles and touch-sized
controls keep the next task easy to find. Counter is Home in both display modes;
Shop overview remains a separate destination.

The public homepage preserves the owner’s sky video and Inter/Instrument Serif
hero pairing. The product story uses actual app screenshots with fictional demo
records and the existing product-photo library. Avoid invented dashboards,
metrics, reviews and customer claims.

Use white, cool slate (#f1f5fb), dark ink (#0b0f1a), and blue (#2563eb).
Small blue text and blue buttons use darker variants for contrast. The closing
signup section uses navy (#15243e) and light text. Sections share a continuous
page rather than repeated rounded panels. Device frames keep screenshots legible.

Landing styles stay scoped under `.site`. The hero has content-driven height so
it cannot hide the primary action on short screens. Mobile navigation closes on
selection or Escape. The email signup stacks on narrow phones. Preserve semantic
headings, visible focus and the video’s reduced-motion/data-saver fallback.
