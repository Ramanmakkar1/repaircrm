# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Repair-shop owners, service advisors, technicians, and front-desk staff who need
to coordinate customer requests and repair work while serving people in person.
They need to understand the shop's priorities quickly without learning a complex
back-office system.

## Product Purpose

RepairPilot manages the repair-shop workflow from lead and appointment through
work order, estimate, invoice, payment, inventory, and customer handoff. Success
means staff can see what needs attention, move work forward, and keep customers
informed from one dependable system.

## Brand Personality

Confident, practical, and clear. The experience should feel dependable under
time pressure and approachable for small teams. The user named Stripe, PhonePe,
and Semrush as quality references, with an emphasis on professional software
craft and quick navigation.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded, unorganized CRM dashboards; hidden primary actions; decorative
charts that do not help staff decide what to do; and status cues that rely on
color alone. Platform administration must be clearly separate from an individual
shop's everyday workspace.

## Design Principles

- Put the next useful action and the work needing attention first.
- Keep repair status, ownership, and customer context easy to scan together.
- Use consistent navigation and labels across the shop's workflow.
- Show real operational data and make missing integrations or unavailable
  measurements clear.
- Keep account-wide administrative controls separate from shop operations and
  require explicit access checks for them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for new and revised product surfaces. Preserve keyboard
operation and visible focus, use semantic labels and headings, avoid using color
as the only status signal, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
